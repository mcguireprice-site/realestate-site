Buy

Buying in Chicago, building by building.

A listing shows you the photos and the square footage. My job is to help you understand the rest: the building, the association, the block, and whether the home fits the next several years of your life.

01

What a listing doesn't tell you

Five things I look at before you make an offer.

  • The homeWhich way the windows face, how the floor plan works with furniture in it, and what the condition says about the next ten years.
  • The buildingThe association's budget and reserves, past and planned special assessments, rental rules, the share of units rented, and any litigation. The condo guide
  • The blockWhat the street is like at night, what's planned for the empty lot, and how far the walk to the train really is in January. Neighborhood guides
  • The numbersComparable sales in the building and nearby, what the dues cover, property taxes, and what the home costs all-in each month.
  • What's nextWhether it still works if your plans change, and how it will compare when you sell.
02

Questions I ask about every condo

In Illinois, most of the answers are in the seller's condo disclosure documents.

  1. How much is in reserves, and when was the last reserve study?

  2. Have there been special assessments in the last five years, and are any planned?

  3. What major work is coming: roof, masonry, facade, elevators, windows?

  4. What share of units are rented, and is there a rental cap?

  5. Does the building meet lender requirements for conventional and FHA loans?

  6. Is the association involved in any lawsuits?

  7. What do the dues cover: heat, gas, internet, parking?

  8. Is the parking space deeded, leased or assigned?

The seller provides most of this under Section 22.1 of the Illinois Condominium Property Act. I read it with you before you're committed, not after. Read the full guide →

How we'll work together

  1. Clarify

    Budget, timeline, must-haves and the neighborhoods worth considering, plus a lender conversation if you haven't had one.

  2. Search

    We tour homes that fit, and I'll tell you what I see, including the reasons a home might not be right for you.

  3. Evaluate

    Comparable sales and, for a condo, the association's documents, before any offer.

  4. Offer and close

    I negotiate for you and keep things on track through attorney review, inspection, financing and closing.
