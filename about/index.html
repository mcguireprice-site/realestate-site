About McGuire Price

A broker who starts with the building.

I grew up in Alabama and came to Chicago for Northwestern, where I studied communications and theatre, with business institutions and film studies alongside. Theatre trains you to read a space: how a room is entered, where the light falls, what a set says before anyone speaks.

Chicago's buildings did the rest. The greystones, the courtyard buildings and Mies van der Rohe's towers on Lake Shore Drive kept pulling my attention. Real estate turned that into something useful for other people.

“I'd rather be the person you call with a question two years before you move than the one who shows up only at the closing table.”

01

How I work

Most of my clients aren't buying anything unusual. They're buying a first condo, selling a two-flat, or trying to decide whether Lakeview or Ravenswood makes more sense for the next five years.

My approach is the same either way: look closely, ask the questions people tend to skip, and give you a straight answer, even when the answer is to wait.

  • I'll tell you when to wait.Sometimes the right move is not this unit, or not this year.
  • I read the documents.Budgets, reserves, board minutes, rules. The boring pages are where the surprises are.
  • I answer questions whether or not you're buying.A quick call now is worth more than a rushed decision later.
