Where I work

Nine neighborhoods, each with its own housing stock and its own questions to ask. A few highlights:

  • Lincoln Park

    Victorian greystones, brick row houses and vintage courtyard buildings around the park and the lakefront.

  • Gold Coast

    Mansions and townhouses in the Astor Street landmark district, and prewar co-ops and condos facing the lake.

  • Streeterville

    Lakefront towers, including Mies van der Rohe's 860–880 North Lake Shore Drive, steps from Michigan Avenue.

  • West Loop

    Converted warehouse lofts beside new construction, around the restaurant corridor of Fulton Market.
