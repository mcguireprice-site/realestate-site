01

Everything the listing leaves out

Photos and square footage are where a listing stops. This is what I look at before anyone makes a decision.

  • The home

    Floor plan, light, exposure, condition, parking, outdoor space: what living there is actually like.

  • The building

    How it was built, how it has been kept up, and how the association runs it.

  • The block

    What's on the street, what's going up nearby, and how it feels on a weeknight.

  • The numbers

    Comparable sales, dues, reserves, assessments and taxes, read together.

  • What's next

    How the home fits your next five years, and how it will look to the next buyer.
