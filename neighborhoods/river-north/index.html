River North, Chicago

Loft buildings, modern towers, galleries and dining, just north of the river from the Loop.

01

The neighborhood

River North was once a district of warehouses and factories along the Chicago River. Many of those buildings became lofts, and the area grew into a center for galleries, design showrooms and restaurants.

The Merchandise Mart anchors the riverfront, and the Chicago Riverwalk is steps away.

02

River North, section by section

How the neighborhood changes from one part to the next.

  • The riverfrontTowers facing the main branch of the river, including Marina City, with skyline and river views and the Merchandise Mart nearby.
  • The loft districtAround Superior, Huron and Franklin: former warehouses converted to lofts, many of them once home to galleries.
  • Hubbard and KinzieThe busiest nightlife blocks, with restaurants and bars below newer towers.
03

Homes you'll find

The housing you'll see most often in River North.

  • Loft conversionsTimber and brick lofts in former industrial buildings.
  • High-rise condo towersMany with river or skyline views.
  • Boutique mid-risesSmaller condo buildings on the side streets.
  • Rental-heavy newer buildingsWorth knowing about for financing and resale.
04

Buildings worth knowing

Architecture and landmarks I point out on tours.

  • Marina City

    Bertrand Goldberg's 1960s "corncob" towers, still residential condos.

  • Merchandise Mart

    When it opened in 1930, it was the largest building in the world by floor area.

  • Driehaus Museum

    Samuel Nickerson's 1883 mansion, with some of the city's finest Gilded Age interiors.

05

Real estate notes

What's specific about buying and owning in River North.

  • Many lofts have interior sleeping areas without windows. Whether a room counts as a legal bedroom affects value and resale.
  • Several newer towers are mostly rentals, which can limit financing for condo units in them.
  • On some blocks, nightlife is loud late. Visit at the hours you'd actually be home.
