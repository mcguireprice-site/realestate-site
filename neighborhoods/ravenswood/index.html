Ravenswood, Chicago

Quiet, tree-lined streets of single-family homes and two-flats, with an easy commute downtown.

Housing
Single-family homes and two-flats, with vintage condos

01

The neighborhood

Ravenswood began in the 1860s as one of Chicago's first commuter suburbs, built around a railroad stop that is now Metra's Union Pacific North Line.

Lincoln Square's shops and restaurants are to the west, and the Ravenswood Avenue corridor along the tracks is home to breweries, makers and small businesses.

02

Ravenswood, section by section

How the neighborhood changes from one part to the next.

  • Along the MetraRavenswood Avenue follows the tracks, with former industrial buildings now home to breweries, makers and small businesses.
  • The residential blocksEast and west of the tracks: Victorian-era houses, two-flats and newer homes on deep, tree-lined lots, around parks like Winnemac.
  • Toward Lincoln SquareCloser to Western and Lawrence: Lincoln Square's shops and restaurants, and more vintage condo buildings.
03

Homes you'll find

The housing you'll see most often in Ravenswood.

  • Victorian and early-1900s housesFrame and brick single-family homes, many on generous lots.
  • Two-flats and three-flatsPopular with buyers who want rental income.
  • Vintage courtyard condosScattered throughout.
  • Newer constructionSingle-family homes and small condo buildings.
04

Buildings worth knowing

Architecture and landmarks I point out on tours.

  • Ravenswood Metra station

    The reason the neighborhood exists.

  • Victorian homes

    Some of the North Side's earliest suburban houses.

  • Rosehill Cemetery gate

    Just to the north, a castle-like 1864 entrance by W.W. Boyington, architect of the Water Tower.

05

Real estate notes

What's specific about buying and owning in Ravenswood.

  • Many houses are over a century old. Electrical, plumbing, foundations and basement water are the usual questions.
  • Two-flats are a common way to buy here with rental income. Confirm the units are legal and separately metered.
  • Homes next to the Metra embankment can feel very different from those a few doors away.
