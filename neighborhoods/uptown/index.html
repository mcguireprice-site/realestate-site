Uptown, Chicago

Historic music venues, lakefront parks and some of the city's most beautiful vintage housing, all along the Red Line.

01

The neighborhood

Uptown was one of Chicago's entertainment centers in the 1920s, and its theaters and ballrooms are still a big part of its identity. The Green Mill, the Aragon Ballroom and the Riviera all draw crowds.

Beyond the music venues, Uptown holds some of the North Side's best vintage architecture: terra cotta commercial buildings along Broadway, large Queen Anne homes and early apartment buildings in Buena Park and Sheridan Park, and lakefront parks around Montrose Harbor.

02

Uptown, section by section

How the neighborhood changes from one part to the next.

  • Buena ParkThe southeast corner, near Graceland Cemetery and the lake: large Queen Anne and early-1900s homes and handsome vintage buildings.
  • Sheridan ParkWest of Broadway: a historic district of homes and early apartment buildings on quiet, tree-lined streets.
  • Broadway and LawrenceThe entertainment district: the Green Mill, the Aragon, the Riviera and the Uptown Theatre, with terra cotta commercial buildings along Broadway.
  • Marine Drive and the lakefrontHigh-rises facing Lincoln Park and Montrose Harbor, many from the mid-century.
03

Homes you'll find

The housing you'll see most often in Uptown.

  • Vintage courtyard and walk-up condosMany with original woodwork, sunrooms and courtyard gardens.
  • Queen Anne and Victorian homesConcentrated in Buena Park and Sheridan Park.
  • Greystones and two-flatsThroughout the neighborhood.
  • Lakefront high-risesCondos and co-ops along Marine Drive and Sheridan Road.
04

Buildings worth knowing

Architecture and landmarks I point out on tours.

  • Graceland Cemetery

    Louis Sullivan's Getty Tomb, and the graves of Sullivan, Daniel Burnham and Mies van der Rohe. Owning in a Mies building

  • Uptown Theatre

    A vast 1925 movie palace that has been closed for decades.

  • Montrose Point Bird Sanctuary

    The "Magic Hedge," a well-known stop for migrating birds.

  • Buena Park and Sheridan Park

    Historic districts of large homes and early apartment buildings.

05

Real estate notes

What's specific about buying and owning in Uptown.

  • Vintage condo quality varies building by building. Look at reserves, recent masonry and roof work, and how many units are rented.
  • New rental buildings have gone up near the rebuilt Red Line stations. Check what's planned near any address.
  • Historic district status in Buena Park and Sheridan Park can affect exterior changes.
