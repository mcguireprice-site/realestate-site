Neighborhoods

Chicago, neighborhood by neighborhood.

These are the neighborhoods where I work most often, each with its own character and pace of life.

  • 01

    Lincoln Park

    Classic Chicago homes and condos near the park, the lake and the zoo.

  • 02

    Lakeview

    A lively mix of vintage buildings, two-flats and lakefront high-rises.

  • 03

    Gold Coast

    Historic homes and elegant lakefront buildings, close to downtown.

  • 04

    Old Town

    Charming, walkable streets and historic homes near downtown.

  • 05

    Streeterville

    Lakefront high-rise living, steps from Michigan Avenue.

  • 06

    West Loop

    Lofts and new construction near Fulton Market's restaurants.

  • 07

    Ravenswood

    Quiet, tree-lined streets of single-family homes and two-flats.

  • 08

    River North

    Loft buildings and modern towers near the river, galleries and dining.

  • 09

    South Loop

    Lofts and newer high-rises near the lakefront and the Museum Campus.
