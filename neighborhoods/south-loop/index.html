South Loop, Chicago

Lofts, townhomes and high-rises near Grant Park, the lakefront and the Museum Campus.

Housing
Lofts, high-rise condos, townhomes and some single-family homes
Typical buildings
Printer's Row loft conversions, high-rises from several decades, townhome communities
Often a fit for
Buyers who want lakefront and museum access, downtown convenience and a wide range of price points
Compare with
West Loop, Streeterville
01

The neighborhood

The South Loop has changed as much as any part of the city over the past few decades. Former rail yards and printing plants became residential neighborhoods.

Today it's home to the Museum Campus, Soldier Field and the south end of Grant Park, with the lakefront an easy walk away.

02

South Loop, section by section

How the neighborhood changes from one part to the next.

  • Printer's RowDearborn Street and Plymouth Court south of Congress: loft conversions in former printing buildings, with Dearborn Station at the south end.
  • Michigan and Indiana avenuesHigh-rise condos facing Grant Park and the lake, from older towers to newer construction.
  • Prairie District and Dearborn ParkTownhome communities, some single-family homes, and the Prairie Avenue historic district with Glessner House.
  • Near Museum CampusNewer towers near Roosevelt Road and the lakefront, close to the Field Museum, Shedd Aquarium and Soldier Field.
03

Homes you'll find

The housing you'll see most often in South Loop.

  • Loft conversionsMany in Printer's Row's former printing buildings.
  • High-rise condosAlong Michigan and Indiana avenues and near the lakefront.
  • Townhomes and single-family homesIn Dearborn Park and the Prairie District.
  • Newer constructionTowers and mid-rises built over the last two decades.
04

Buildings worth knowing

Architecture and landmarks I point out on tours.

  • Printing House Row District

    A landmark district of loft buildings from the printing trade.

  • Glessner House

    H.H. Richardson's 1887 house in the Prairie Avenue District.

  • Dearborn Station

    Chicago's oldest surviving train station, from 1885.

05

Real estate notes

What's specific about buying and owning in South Loop.

  • Townhome communities often have a master association on top of the unit's own. Read both sets of rules and budgets.
  • Building ages range from the 1890s to the last few years. Compare reserves, rental share and any litigation building by building.
  • Soldier Field and McCormick Place events affect traffic and parking on nearby blocks.

How it compares

If you're also looking at…

07

Getting around

  • Red LineHarrison, Roosevelt and Cermak-Chinatown stations.
  • Green and Orange LinesRoosevelt station, plus Cermak-McCormick Place on the Green Line.
  • MetraMuseum Campus/11th Street on the Metra Electric and South Shore lines, plus LaSalle Street Station.
  • DrivingLake Shore Drive and quick expressway access.
