Neighborhoods / Edgewater

Edgewater

Lakefront high-rises, vintage courtyard buildings and Andersonville's shops and restaurants on the far North Side.

01

The neighborhood

Edgewater runs along the lake north of Foster Avenue. Its eastern side is lined with high-rises and co-ops along Sheridan Road, where Lake Shore Drive comes to an end.

To the west is Andersonville, a lively stretch of Clark Street with Swedish roots, independent shops and restaurants. In between are some of the city's quietest blocks of large single-family homes.

02

Homes you'll find

The housing you'll see most often in Edgewater.

  • Lakefront high-rise condos and co-opsMany with direct lake views.
  • Vintage courtyard buildingsThroughout, including well-kept 1920s apartment hotels.
  • Large single-family homesIn Lakewood Balmoral and Edgewater Glen.
  • Two-flats and greystonesEspecially around Andersonville.
03

Getting around

  • Red LineBerwyn, Bryn Mawr, Thorndale and Granville stations.
  • DrivingLake Shore Drive begins at Hollywood Avenue.
  • LakefrontOsterman Beach and the north end of the Lakefront Trail.
04

Worth a look

Architecture and landmarks I point out on tours.

  • Edgewater Beach Apartments

    The pink 1929 tower at 5555 N. Sheridan Road, a neighborhood icon.

  • Bryn Mawr Historic District

    1920s apartment hotels, including the Belle Shore and the Bryn Mawr.

  • Andersonville

    Clark Street's commercial district and the Swedish American Museum.

  • Lakewood Balmoral

    Blocks of large early-1900s homes.
