Edgewater, Chicago

01

The neighborhood

Edgewater runs along the lake north of Foster Avenue. Its eastern side is lined with high-rises and co-ops along Sheridan Road, where Lake Shore Drive comes to an end.

To the west is Andersonville, a lively stretch of Clark Street with Swedish roots, independent shops and restaurants. In between are some of the city's quietest blocks of large single-family homes.

02

Edgewater, section by section

How the neighborhood changes from one part to the next.

  • Sheridan RoadThe lakefront corridor: high-rises and co-ops, many with direct lake views, where Lake Shore Drive ends.
  • Bryn Mawr Historic DistrictBryn Mawr Avenue near the Red Line: 1920s apartment hotels such as the Belle Shore and the Bryn Mawr.
  • AndersonvilleClark Street's shops and restaurants, with two-flats, greystones and vintage condos on the surrounding blocks.
  • Lakewood Balmoral and Edgewater GlenQuiet blocks of large early-1900s single-family homes on wider lots.
03

Homes you'll find

The housing you'll see most often in Edgewater.

  • Lakefront high-rise condos and co-opsMany with direct lake views.
  • Vintage courtyard buildingsThroughout, including well-kept 1920s apartment hotels.
  • Large single-family homesIn Lakewood Balmoral and Edgewater Glen.
  • Two-flats and greystonesEspecially around Andersonville.
04

Buildings worth knowing

Architecture and landmarks I point out on tours.

  • Edgewater Beach Apartments

    The pink 1929 tower at 5555 N. Sheridan Road, a neighborhood icon.

  • Bryn Mawr Historic District

    1920s apartment hotels, including the Belle Shore and the Bryn Mawr.

  • Andersonville

    Clark Street's commercial district and the Swedish American Museum.

  • Lakewood Balmoral

    Blocks of large early-1900s homes.

05

Real estate notes

What's specific about buying and owning in Edgewater.

  • Co-ops are common along Sheridan Road. They can be priced below comparable condos, but come with board approval, specific lending rules and sometimes a larger down payment.
  • In the lakefront towers, look at reserves, special assessments and any facade work.
  • Single-family homes in Lakewood Balmoral and Edgewater Glen come up for sale rarely.
