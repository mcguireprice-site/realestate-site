Neighborhoods / Streeterville

Streeterville, Chicago

Lakefront high-rise living between Michigan Avenue and the lake, with hospitals, museums and Navy Pier at the door.

Housing
Almost entirely high-rise condos and rentals
Typical buildings
Mid-century and modernist towers, 1980s and 1990s high-rises, newer luxury towers
Often a fit for
Buyers who want lake views, building amenities and a short walk to work, hospitals or the Loop
Compare with
Gold Coast, River North
01

The neighborhood

Streeterville is almost entirely high-rise, and one of the most walkable places to live in Chicago.

Northwestern University's downtown campus and Northwestern Memorial Hospital are here, along with the Museum of Contemporary Art and Navy Pier. Ohio Street Beach and the Lakefront Trail are minutes away.

02

Streeterville, section by section

How the neighborhood changes from one part to the next.

  • The lakefrontTowers along Lake Shore Drive, including Mies van der Rohe's 860–880 and 900–910, with direct lake views and some of the city's most important modern buildings.
  • Around Northwestern MemorialThe hospital and university campus at the center of the neighborhood, surrounded by rental and condo towers. Busy on weekdays.
  • East of Michigan AvenueTowers between Michigan Avenue and the lake, with retail, hotels and Water Tower Place close by.
  • Toward Navy PierNewer towers along Grand and Illinois, closer to the pier, the river and Ohio Street Beach.
03

Homes you'll find

The housing you'll see most often in Streeterville.

  • Lakefront condo towersMany with open views of the lake, Navy Pier and the skyline.
  • Prewar and mid-century high-risesIncluding some of the city's most important modern buildings.
  • Newer luxury towersWith larger floor plans and more amenities.
  • Rental-heavy buildingsSome towers have a high share of rented units, which affects financing.
04

Buildings worth knowing

Architecture and landmarks I point out on tours.

  • 860–880 Lake Shore Drive

    Mies van der Rohe's 1951 glass-and-steel towers, which shaped high-rise design worldwide. Owning in a Mies building

  • Lake Point Tower

    The curved glass tower east of Lake Shore Drive, finished in 1968.

  • Tribune Tower

    The 1925 Gothic Revival tower, now converted to residences.

05

Real estate notes

What's specific about buying and owning in Streeterville.

  • In a large tower, the building is half the purchase: reserves, special assessments, facade and garage work, and rental and pet rules.
  • Buildings with a high share of rented units can be harder to finance. Ask early whether the building meets conventional and FHA lending guidelines.
  • Parking is often sold or leased separately. Know which before you compare prices.

How it compares

If you're also looking at…

  • Gold CoastMore historic streets and prewar co-ops. Streeterville is newer and more amenity-driven.
  • River NorthMore lofts, nightlife and new rental towers. Streeterville is more residential and closer to the lake.
07

Getting around

  • Red LineGrand and Chicago stations, a short walk west.
  • BusesFrequent service on Michigan Avenue and Lake Shore Drive.
