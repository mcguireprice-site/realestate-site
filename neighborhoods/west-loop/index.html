Neighborhoods / West Loop

West Loop, Chicago

Converted warehouses, new towers and Chicago's busiest restaurant scene, just across the river from the Loop.

Housing
Lofts and new-construction condos, with townhomes and some older homes
Typical buildings
Timber and concrete loft conversions, new mid-rise and high-rise condos, newer row homes
Often a fit for
Buyers who want restaurants, newer construction, offices nearby and easy expressway access
Compare with
South Loop, River North
01

The neighborhood

The West Loop was a district of meatpackers, produce markets and factories. Today Fulton Market and Randolph Street hold many of the city's most talked-about restaurants, alongside a growing number of corporate offices.

Mary Bartelme and Skinner parks give the residential blocks some green space.

02

West Loop, section by section

How the neighborhood changes from one part to the next.

  • Fulton MarketThe former meatpacking district: converted warehouses, new office and residential towers, and many of the city's most talked-about restaurants.
  • Randolph Street and the central West LoopRestaurant Row and the blocks around it, with loft conversions and newer condo buildings.
  • Greektown and HalstedAround Halsted south of Madison: condos and lofts near the University of Illinois Chicago and the Blue Line.
  • Around Skinner and Bartelme parksThe most residential part: townhomes, newer single-family homes and mid-rise condos near the parks and schools.
03

Homes you'll find

The housing you'll see most often in West Loop.

  • Loft conversionsTimber and concrete lofts in former warehouses and factories.
  • New-construction condosMid-rise and high-rise buildings, many built in the last decade.
  • TownhomesNewer row homes, often with garages and roof decks.
  • Older homesA smaller number of greystones and single-family houses.
04

Buildings worth knowing

Architecture and landmarks I point out on tours.

  • Fulton-Randolph Market District

    A landmark district protecting the area's market buildings.

  • Union Station

    Its 1925 Great Hall is one of the city's great interiors.

  • Old St. Patrick's Church

    Dedicated in 1856, it survived the Great Chicago Fire.

05

Real estate notes

What's specific about buying and owning in West Loop.

  • A true timber-loft conversion and a newer "soft loft" can look alike in photos and age very differently.
  • Construction is constant. Check what is planned on neighboring lots before paying for a view.
  • Blocks near the Kennedy, the Metra tracks or the Green Line hear them. Visit at rush hour.
