Chicago home valuation

What would your home sell for today?

Online estimates can't see your floor plan, your view, your parking or your building's finances. I can. Tell me about your home and I'll prepare a market analysis based on recent sales and the details that actually move the price.

What you'll get

  • A suggested price range and the reasoning behind it
  • The recent sales I compared it with, and why those
  • For condos: how your unit compares within the building and with similar buildings nearby
  • What could raise or lower the number, from small repairs to timing
  • No obligation to list, now or later

How it works

  1. Tell me about your home. It takes about two minutes.
  2. I review recent sales and may ask a few questions, or suggest a quick visit if it would change the answer.
  3. You get your analysis, and we can talk it through.

Questions people ask

How is this different from Zillow or Redfin?

Their estimates come from public records and nearby sales. They don't know your unit's exposure, your parking, your renovation or your association's reserves, and in condo buildings those often decide the price.

Is this an appraisal?

No. An appraisal is ordered by a lender during a purchase. This is a broker's market analysis: what I'd expect the home to sell for, and why.

Do I have to meet you or list with you?

No. Most people start with the analysis and a phone call. Plenty of people ask a year or two before they plan to sell.
